NOCTURNE
is a solo project

Based between Berlin and the internet, NOCTURNE is the alias of a producer who builds music from industrial scrap: field recordings from factory floors, detuned machinery, magnetic tape artifacts, and raw synthesis.

The work sits at the intersection of techno, EBM, and industrial. Hard drums, hypnotic repetition, and an obsessive attention to texture define the sound — music designed for movement in dark rooms, or solitary late-night listening.

All tracks are produced with a hardware-first approach. No VSTs. No shortcuts.
